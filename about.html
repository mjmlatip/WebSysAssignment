Who we are

Curious minds with a love for the ocean.

Blue Horizon started as a group of students who wanted to learn more about marine life. Today, we share simple lessons, beach activities, and stories about our beautiful ocean.

Our goal is simple: help people enjoy the sea while making choices that keep it healthy for future generations.
